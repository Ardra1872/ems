1. Authentication and role-based access

Login for Admin, Manager and Employee. Each role gets different pages and permissions.



2. Employee management

Add, view, edit and deactivate employee records, including department, designation and contact details.



3. Project and team management

Create projects, assign managers, organize teams and associate employees with projects.



4. Task management

Assign tasks, set deadlines and track Pending, In Progress and Completed tasks.



5. Attendance and leave

Record daily attendance, view attendance history and manage leave requests.



6. Admin dashboard and reports

Employee count, task completion rate, attendance trends and project summaries using charts.
